Why the total grows quickly

At a fixed retry count, doubling the base delay doubles the total wait. The first retry adds one base delay. After that, each additional retry adds twice the previous retry's wait.

Total wait = base_delay × (2retries − 1)

With 3 retries, the multiplier is 7: a 1 s base delay gives 7 s of total waiting.

Model assumptions

  • Retry k waits base_delay * 2^(k-1) seconds; k starts at 1.
  • The initial attempt has no preceding wait. Total attempts = retries + 1.
  • All selected retries are counted. Only their waiting time is included; execution time is excluded.
  • Base delay is 0.5–3 seconds in 0.5-second steps. Retry count is 0–5.

A local calculation only. No network calls, timers, or real retries.
